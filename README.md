# Emergency Savings Runway Months Planner

Enter your monthly expenses, current savings, and monthly savings rate to see how many months of runway you have. Select a 3, 6, 9, or 12 month emergency fund target to see the gap, progress bar, and estimated weeks to reach your goal.

## Features

- Current runway calculation in months based on expenses and savings
- Target fund selector: 3, 6, 9, or 12 months of expenses
- Gap-to-target amount with progress bar visualization
- Time-to-target estimate in weeks and months at current savings rate
